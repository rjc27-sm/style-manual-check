Ask the Style Manual ✦

Ask a question – ‘is it 5 pm or 5pm?’, ‘when do I write numbers as words?’ – and get a short answer with a link to the relevant Style Manual page. Answers are AI-generated: check the linked guidance before relying on them. Don't include sensitive or personal information in your questions.

AI features are not switched on for this copy of the tool, so the chat is unavailable. The document checker, list formatter and citation generator all work without AI.

About this bot

Proof Positive (IM2026) is an entry in the APS Innovation Month 2026 ‘Build a Bureaucrat Bot’ challenge. It is a personal project, not an official government service, and it does not replace editorial judgement or the Australian Government Style Manual.

Your data

Questions on this page are sent to the Claude API (Anthropic) to generate an answer; they are not used to train models. Don't enter classified, sensitive or personal information.

Answers are drawn from extracts of the actual Style Manual pages, not the AI's general knowledge, and can only link to the pages it read – the bot cannot invent rules or references.
