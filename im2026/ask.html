About this bot

Proof Positive (IM2026) is an entry in the APS Innovation Month 2026 ‘Build a Bureaucrat Bot’ challenge. It is a personal project, not an official government service, and it does not replace editorial judgement or the Australian Government Style Manual.

Your data

This page sends questions to the Claude API (Anthropic) to generate an answer. Your questions are not used to train models. Don't enter classified, sensitive or personal information.

Page visits and AI requests are counted anonymously, so the reach of the tool can be reported. No text is recorded and nothing is stored on your device. What is counted.

Answers are drawn from extracts of the actual Style Manual pages, not the AI's general knowledge. The bot provides links to the pages it read.
