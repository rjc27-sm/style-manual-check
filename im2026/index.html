Check a document

Proof Positive checks your writing against the Australian Government Style Manual using a deterministic rule engine. The same check gives the same answer every time. Where fixing an issue takes judgement, generative AI drafts a rewrite and the rule engine checks the output before you see it.

Rule checks run entirely in your browser – your document never leaves your browser. AI features (marked ✦) send only the text you choose to the Claude API. Don't enter classified, sensitive or personal information.

About this bot

Proof Positive (IM2026) is an entry in the APS Innovation Month 2026 ‘Build a Bureaucrat Bot’ challenge. It is a personal project, not an official government service, and it does not replace editorial judgement or the Australian Government Style Manual.

Your data

Rule engine checks run entirely in your browser. Features marked with ✦ send the text you choose to the Claude API (Anthropic) to generate a suggestion; it is not used to train models. Don't enter classified, sensitive or personal information.

Rules based on the Australian Government Style Manual, © Commonwealth of Australia, used under CC BY 4.0. Deterministic rule engine – AI is only used where judgement is needed, and its output is re-checked against the rules.
