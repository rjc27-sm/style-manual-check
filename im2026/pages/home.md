- Sentence case, title case and the titles of works Writers often refer to the titles of published and unpublished material (works) in their content. There are different rules for styling the titles of various types of works such as books, magazines and podcasts. The rules use capitalisation (sentence case and title case), punctuation, and italic or roman type to help users identify titles easily. 18 September 2025

### Sentence case, title case and the titles of works

Writers often refer to the titles of published and unpublished material (works) in their content. There are different rules for styling the titles of various types of works such as books, magazines and podcasts. The rules use capitalisation (sentence case and title case), punctuation, and italic or roman type to help users identify titles easily.

18 September 2025
