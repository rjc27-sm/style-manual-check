Regional Grants Program Guidelines

Eligibility and Application Process

Applicants must meet the following criteria:

- Be an Australian citizen or permanent resident;
- Have a current ABN,
- Demonstrate financial viability;
- Provide evidence of community support; and
- etc.

The program operates on a biweekly assessment cycle. Applications close at 12 pm on the final Friday of each month. Late submissions received after 5.30 pm will not be considered. The information session runs from 10 am in the morning until 2.00 pm in the afternoon.

Key dates for the 2025-26 program year:

- Round 1 opens: 15 January
- Round 1 closes: 28 February
- Assessment period: March to April
- outcomes announced: May
- Round 2 opens: 1 July

The timezone for all deadlines is AEST 17:00, and applicants in other states should adjust accordingly. The grant was first offered in the 1990's and has been revised for the 2019/20 period.

Assessment Criteria

Applications are assessed against the following criteria:

- Strategic alignment with government priorities.
- demonstrated community need and demand.
- Organisational capacity to deliver.
- Value for money.

The panel noted that the 'criteria,' listed above, should be read in conjunction with the program guidelines. The panel / secretariat will review all submissions. An en dash is used for ranges like pages 10 - 20 in the supporting documentation.

Eligible activities include:

- Community infrastructure projects
- Training and workforce development
- Research and evaluation
- Equipment purchases

Funding Amounts

Small grants of up to $50,000 are available for community organisations. Medium grants of $50,000 to $200,000 support regional partnerships. Large grants over $200,000 require ministerial approval.

The program received bimonthly progress reports throughout the year. Each report covers the period ending at 12 am on the last day of the reporting month.
