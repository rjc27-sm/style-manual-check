Policy Briefing: Modernising Regional Service Delivery

Background And Context

The Commonwealth government announced a new strategy to modernize and optimize service delivery across regional Australia. The Minister of Health and Aged Care has emphasized that the Department must prioritize this initiative going forward.

The judgment of the review panel, released January 15, 2025, concluded that the organization had failed to recognize the need for modernization. It recommends that we analyze current practices, utilize new technologies, and fulfill our commitments to regional communities. The defense of the current approach is no longer tenable.

Key Findings

The review — conducted over 6 months — identified several areas for improvement. The programme of works has not delivered the anticipated benefits, and could of been better managed from the outset. A total of 14 travelers were surveyed across 3 centers, and each fiber of the organisation's structure was examined.

The panel noted that practice was not centred on evidence. For example, the labeling of pilot sites was inconsistent, and modeling of outcomes relied on gray literature rather than peer-reviewed sources.

The Minister said the Government would act on the recommendations.  He noted that "the current approach is unsustainable" and urged the Department to take action. The secretary confirmed that the Department would work to fulfill all 23 recommendations.

Regarding the 05/03/2025 deadline, the panel expressed concern. The 1st phase should commence by 3rd March and conclude no later than the 2nd quarter. An interim report is expected on the 21st of June.

The review found that the 1980's model of service delivery is outdated. Meetings are held on Thursday, 15 May each year. The 2018/19 financial year saw a significant decline in performance.

Next Steps.

The Minister has requested that the Department develop a comprehensive implementation plan. The Agency will be responsible for coordinating across government. This plan must acknowledge the seriousness of the situation and endeavour to address all shortcomings identified in the review.
